Click Before Frames
Shifts your inputs backwards in simulated time by a configurable number of physics ticks
(1 tick = 1/240 s on GD 2.2081).
It captures a snapshot of the simulation every step, and when you press, it rewinds the
world by N ticks, re-asserts your input, and lets the tick run again — so your click
exists earlier in world time than the frame you actually clicked on.
This is a cheat, not an input-precision tool. It changes when your input happened
relative to the level, and it is not leaderboard-safe.
Requires the "Snapshot Rewind" setting to be enabled; with it off the mod only measures and
reports, and changes nothing.
